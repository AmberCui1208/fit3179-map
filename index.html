Australian Fishery Value Analysis

Australia’s fisheries are a vital part of the nation’s economy and food security. This dashboard shows how values change across states and territories, highlighting regional strengths and long-term trends.

Explore the patterns to identify growth hotspots, emerging opportunities, and the evolving landscape of Australia’s seafood industry.

Designed for a general audience, this interactive Vega-Lite dashboard turns official statistics into clear visuals that aid understanding and policy reflection.

Key Insights · Summary
